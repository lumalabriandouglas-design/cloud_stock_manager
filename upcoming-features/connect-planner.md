# Connect shop ↔ personal planner (not built)

The other app (spend / plan your own life) is a **different book**.

Shop cash and personal cash should not mix unless the owner chooses.

## Default

- Cloud Stock Manager = the business
- Planner = rent, food, airtime, goals
- Two logins can be the same Google, but two ledgers

## If a client wants them connected

Owner taps **Connect this shop** in the planner (or **Share totals** in the shop).

Share only what helps planning, not the whole inventory:

- Today’s sales total
- Money customers still owe
- Money the shop still owes
- Optional: “pay yourself” from shop profit

Do **not** dump every product and every sale into the personal app. That would crowd a life planner and leak staff data.

## How the match works later

1. Same person signs in with the same Google / email on both.
2. They pick which shop to attach (one person may own more than one).
3. Planner reads **summaries** from the shop (with permission).
4. Either side can Disconnect. Shop data stays in the shop.

Until both apps are stable, leave this unbuilt. Write sales in the shop. Write personal spend in the planner.
