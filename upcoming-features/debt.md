# Debt (not built)

A shop book for “they owe us” and “we owe them”.

## They owe us

A customer takes goods now and pays later.

- Name, amount, why (which products if easy), date, optional phone
- Can pay part, then the rest later
- When paid, stock already left — this is money coming in, not a new sale unless we recorded it as credit at sell time

## We owe them

Supplier or person gave stock or cash. Shop will pay later.

- Name, amount, why, date
- Mark paid when MoMo / cash goes out

## Simple screen

One list, two filters: **Owed to shop** / **Shop owes**.  
Unpaid totals on Reports.  
Staff who can sell can add a debt. Only the owner can delete.

Keep this in Cloud Stock Manager. It is a shop fact, not a personal budget.
